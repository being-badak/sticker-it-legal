Privacy Policy for Sticker It

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Sticker It ("we", "us") is a free sticker maker and sticker keyboard for Android, published by Doodlebits. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. Questions: patilsarthak101@gmail.com.

In short

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your photos and stickers stay on your phone. We do not receive or store them.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data (see section 3).
  • The optional ad-free subscription is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you create or import is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone: images you add to the Drop Box, the stickers and packs you make, your settings (theme, fonts), and the app's record of whether your subscription is active. We have no server that receives this information. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

2. How images get into the app

Sticker It does not ask for access to your photo library, storage, camera, microphone, contacts or location. Images come in only when you choose them: by sharing an image to Sticker It from another app, by picking photos with Android's system photo picker, or by choosing files with the system file picker.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner and interstitial ads from Google AdMob. According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location)
  • how you interact with ads and the app (for example app launches and taps)
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and hang rate)
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit and is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads) and at adssettings.google.com. Subscribers to the ad-free plan do not see ads.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

The optional ad-free subscription is sold and billed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you have an active subscription, and keeps that yes/no answer on your device so ads stay off when you are offline. We do not receive your name, email or payment information.

You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in Google Play (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

5. The Sticker It keyboard

The keyboard only shows your own stickers and sends the one you tap to the app you are typing in. It does not read, record or store what you type, and it does not send anything over the internet. To send a sticker in a format the other app accepts, the keyboard looks at which image types that app supports; this is not stored.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. The only third parties involved are Google (AdMob for ads and Google Play for purchases). We do not run our own analytics or crash-reporting tools. Google Play may give us anonymous crash and performance reports through the Play Console.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Because your content is stored only on your device, you control it: delete stickers and packs inside the app (deleted stickers sit in the Recycle Bin for 30 days), or uninstall the app to remove everything. We hold no copy and cannot recover deleted items. Data held by Google for advertising or billing is controlled by Google; see the links above.

8. Children

Sticker It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU/UK GDPR) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. We do not hold personal data about you ourselves, so these requests are mostly about data held by Google. You can still write to us and we will help where we can.

10. Changes

If we add features that handle more data (for example a Community with sign-in and sharing), we will update this policy and the in-app copy before the feature collects anything. The date at the top shows the latest version.
