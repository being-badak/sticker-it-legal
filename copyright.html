Copyright & Takedown Policy for Sticker It

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Sticker It respects the rights of creators and copyright owners.

1. How the App handles content

In this version of the App, stickers are made and stored only on the user's own device. Sticker It does not upload, host or publicly distribute users' images or stickers, and we have no access to them. Because of this, we have nothing to take down from our servers for user-made stickers.

If we later add an online feature where people can publish stickers (such as a Community), this policy will apply to that content, and we will update it before the feature launches.

2. If you think the App infringes your copyright

If you are a copyright owner, or are authorised to act for one, and you believe that something in the App itself, its store listing, or any content we host infringes your copyright, email us at patilsarthak101@gmail.com with:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work you say is infringed.
  2. Where the material appears (for example a screenshot or a description of where to find it).
  3. Your name, postal address, phone number and email.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is fine).

3. What we do

We review complete notices promptly. If a notice is valid, we will remove or disable the material we host or control. We may send a copy of the notice to the person responsible, and may keep a record of it.

4. Counter-notice

If you believe material was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may reply with: what was removed and where it appeared; your name, address and contact details; a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification; your consent to the jurisdiction of the courts where you live (or, if outside India, the appropriate courts); and your signature. We may restore the material unless the complainant tells us within a reasonable time that they have started legal action.

5. Repeat infringers

If we host user content in the future, we will end the publishing access of people who repeatedly infringe copyright.

6. Using other people's images

Please only make stickers from images you have the right to use. Using someone else's work without permission can infringe their rights, and you are responsible for the images you use (see our Terms of Service).
